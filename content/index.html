Hello, I’m Nick.

I make software, study interesting problems and occasionally leave the computer.

I’m a software developer and mathematician based in Tasmania. I’ve spent a lot of time working with C++, Go and the web, usually on open-source tools and self-hosted software.

This site is a home for the things I’ve built, the mathematics I’ve worked on, and a few interests that have nothing to do with a terminal.

Background

A slightly unusual route into software

I studied mathematics, economics and applied computing at the University of Tasmania, then completed a PhD in mathematics. Along the way I taught university classes, worked on research projects, and helped organise an international programming competition.

Since then my work has moved increasingly towards software and systems, including operational work at Murray Irrigation and a long-running collection of open-source projects.
